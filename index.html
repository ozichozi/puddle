Puddle Backup

Your data, on the ground.

Back up your Android phone to your own Mac. Photos, videos and the folders you pick go straight to a folder on your Mac, over your own Wi-Fi. No cloud, no account, nothing on anyone's servers.

How it works

  • Install Puddle on your Android phone and on your Mac.
  • Pair them once by scanning a QR code on the Mac.
  • Puddle backs up every night, or whenever you tap Back up now. Files arrive exactly as they are on the phone.
  • Browse what's on your Mac from the phone: photos, videos and documents.

Private by design

Only your phone and Mac can ever read your files: they're encrypted on the way and never kept on anyone's servers. To improve the app, Puddle sends anonymous usage statistics (never your files, names or anything that identifies you, and you can turn them off), plus any feedback you choose to send. Read the privacy policy.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 10 or later.
  • A Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.
  • Both on the same Wi-Fi network when backing up.
